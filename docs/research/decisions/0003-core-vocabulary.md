# 0003: Core vocabulary from Universal Core

Status: accepted as the starter vocabulary until SLPs design the real one. 2026-10-11.

## Context

The plan's biggest gap is a research-grounded open vocabulary ([foundations.md §2.1](../foundations.md#21-a-full-vocabulary-core-plus-fringe)). Until SLPs design it, the starter board was 42 hand-picked words. The [research check](../check-2026-10-11.md) compared them with published lists: they had 28 of Project Core's 36 Universal Core words.

[Universal Core](https://www.med.unc.edu/ahs/clds/universal-core-vocabulary/) is 36 words that UNC's Center for Literacy and Disability Studies prioritised by compiling published core vocabulary lists, reviewing words on speech-generating devices and apps, and checking frequency in written language and academic demands. It's licensed CC BY 4.0, so we can use it with credit.

## Decision

The tablet core board holds all 36 Universal Core words ("finished" as _all done_), plus six from Banajee, DiCarlo and Stricklin's toddler core list ([2003](https://doi.org/10.1080/0743461031000112034)) and early-communicator practice: _my_, _is_, _yes_, _no_, _eat_, _drink_. That fills the 42 word cells exactly.

The approved layout is kept: word types run left to right in sentence order (pronouns, verbs, describing words, little words, questions), the six topic folders sit in the right-hand column, and each word is colour-coded by type.

|     | 1   | 2    | 3    | 4     | 5         | 6        | 7     | 8        |
| --- | --- | ---- | ---- | ----- | --------- | -------- | ----- | -------- |
| 1   | I   | want | get  | is    | more      | that     | not   | People   |
| 2   | you | like | put  | can   | all       | here     | what  | Food     |
| 3   | it  | go   | make | eat   | some      | in       | where | Places   |
| 4   | he  | stop | open | drink | good      | on       | who   | Feelings |
| 5   | she | help | turn | yes   | same      | up       | when  | Body     |
| 6   | my  | look | do   | no    | different | all done | why   | Things   |

Words dropped from the core board: _we, have, again, big, little, bad, down_. _Feel_ moved to the Feelings board and _play_ to Things, where they're used most. The credit Universal Core's licence asks for is in Settings → About and in each core board's description, so it travels with exports.

## Consequences

- Fewer describing words on the home board (_big, little, bad_ are gone); topic boards and editing cover them, and SLPs may want some back.
- Spanish translations were added for the new words: _mi, turno, todo, un poco, igual, diferente, aquí, cuándo, por qué_. _Turno_ is the turn-taking sense ("mi turno"); Universal Core uses _turn_ for both turn-taking and turning things, which Spanish can't do in one word.
- _My_ and _different_ use placeholder drawings until symbols are chosen with SLPs.
- Starter version 7: unedited starter boards update; edited ones don't.
- RELAAACs scoring still waits for the SLP-designed vocabulary.
