# 0005: Phone layouts follow the tablet's order, and can be turned off

Status: accepted. 2026-10-11.

## Context

Each word should have one place, and smaller and larger versions of a vocabulary should share positions ([foundations.md §2.2](../foundations.md#22-motor-planning-consistency)). A 6 × 8 board is too small to touch on a phone, so phones show a 4 × 7 layout, which can't keep the tablet's exact positions. PRC-Saltillo recommends a larger screen and bigger buttons for people starting out ([LAMP WFL on iPhone](https://prc-saltillo.com/blog/LAMPWFL-iPhone)), and Liberator advises starting with the largest grid a person can access ([Liberator](https://liberator.net.au/support/education/aac/grid-sizes)).

## Decision

1. **The phone layout is a subset in matching relative positions.** Its 22 words keep the tablet's left-to-right order (pronouns, verbs, describing words, little words, questions) and, where possible, the same rows; two topic folders sit at the right, like the tablet's folder column, and the other four get a row of their own.
2. **Phone layouts can be turned off** (Settings → Display → Smaller layouts on phones), so someone who uses a phone and a tablet can have one layout everywhere.
3. **[setup.md](../../setup.md) recommends one main device** with a large screen for people starting out.

| Phone | 1      | 2        | 3    | 4        |
| ----- | ------ | -------- | ---- | -------- |
| 1     | I      | want     | more | not      |
| 2     | you    | like     | here | what     |

| 7     | People | Places   | Body | Things   |

## Consequences

The phone layout is still a second set of positions; how much transfers between the two is a question for the vocabulary work with SLPs. The phone layout changed from the approved mockup's to follow the new core vocabulary ([0003](0003-core-vocabulary.md)).
