# 0004: A Spanish-first vocabulary

Status: proposed. 2026-10-11.

## Context

The starter boards' Spanish is a translation of the English-first board, which suits bilingual communicators (same places in both languages, one-tap switching). But word lists don't transfer between languages, and Spanish-speaking children use different words and forms ([findings/languages.md](../findings/languages.md)).

## Proposed decision

Offer a second starter vocabulary chosen in Spanish, from Soto and Cooper's early Spanish list, for Spanish-dominant communicators, selectable when starting or resetting the boards. It is a draft until Spanish-speaking SLPs review it.
