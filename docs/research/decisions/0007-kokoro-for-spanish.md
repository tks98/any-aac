# 0007: Kokoro for a clearly licensed Spanish voice

Status: proposed. 2026-10-11.

## Context

After [0002](0002-only-clearly-licensed-voices.md), the only Open AAC voice in Spanish is Carlfm: male, small and low quality. Kokoro-82M is Apache 2.0, built on the public-domain LJ Speech recordings, and lists Spanish voices, including a female one ([findings/voices.md](../findings/voices.md)).

## Proposed decision

Evaluate Kokoro in the browser (size, speed on an iPhone, Spanish quality, the licence of each Spanish voice) and add it as a second engine if it passes.
