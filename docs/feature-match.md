# Feature match

For SLPs comparing Open AAC with other systems. It follows the categories of the published feature-match checklists (Gosnell 2011; Marfilius & Fonner 2012) used with RELAAACs ([foundations.md §2.11](research/foundations.md#211-what-clinicians-check-in-a-feature-match)). This describes release 0.1.0. The starter vocabularies are built on published research ([decision 0003](research/decisions/0003-core-vocabulary.md), [0004](research/decisions/0004-spanish-first-vocabulary.md)) but haven't yet been reviewed or scored with SLPs.

| Category                     | Open AAC today                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Not yet                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Vocabulary organization**  | Core words on a fixed home board, with topic boards (People, Food, Places, Feelings, Body, Things) that keep core words in the same cells. Word types run left to right (Modified Fitzgerald). Quick phrases board. Any board can be edited or added.                                                           | An SLP-designed vocabulary at several sizes; RELAAACs scoring; keyboard page; visual scene displays. |
| **Symbols**                  | Mulberry Symbols (3,436) and OpenMoji, CC BY-SA 4.0; photos from the camera or files. Symbols can be hidden for text-only boards.                                                                                                                                                                               | Other symbol sets (ARASAAC as an optional download, Global Symbols search).                          |
| **Grid size and navigation** | Home board 6 × 8 (tablet) or 4 × 7 (phone); 1–12 rows and 1–16 columns in edit mode. Folders one level deep in the starter set. Hidden words keep their place.                                                                                                                                                  | A vocabulary-builder workflow for revealing words in stages.                                         |
| **Display**                  | Light, Dark and High contrast themes; colour by word type as fill, border or none; label size; labels above or below symbols; button spacing for keyguards.                                                                                                                                                     | A CVI preset designed with a specialist; font choice.                                                |
| **Access methods**           | Direct touch with hold-to-select, select-on-release and repeat filtering. Switch scanning: automatic, two-switch step and hold-to-move; rows, columns or one at a time; speed, first-item delay and loops; message bar and buttons included; switches as keys, game controllers, tap anywhere or mouse buttons. | Block scanning; two-switch screen halves; dwell; head pointer; eye gaze.                             |
| **Auditory scanning**        | Each item can be said as it's highlighted.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | A separate cue voice; cues in headphones.                                                            |
| **Voices**                   | The device's voices, or offline Open AAC voices (Piper) that sound the same everywhere, with every word's audio prepared ahead. Rate, pitch (device voices), volume, pronunciation dictionary. English and Spanish.                                                                                             | Children's voices; voice banking; a clearly licensed good-quality Spanish voice.                     |
| **Grammar**                  | A Forms button for the last word: verb tenses, plurals, comparatives, pronoun cases in English; present, past, future, gerund, participle, gender and number in Spanish.                                                                                                                                        | Forms that follow the subject already in the message.                                                |
| **Languages**                | Two starter vocabularies: English-first (with Spanish translations) and Spanish-first, chosen in Spanish from published early Spanish vocabulary (with English translations). Board words, voices and menus in English and Spanish; one-tap switching for bilingual communicators; mixed-language messages.     | More languages; right-to-left scripts; SLP review of the Spanish vocabulary.                         |
| **Editing**                  | In place, behind a press-and-hold: labels in each language, spoken text, symbols, word type, links to boards, hide, lock position, copy and paste, swap, undo. Changes save immediately.                                                                                                                        | Drag to swap; cropping photos; recorded sounds; bulk editing.                                        |
| **Teaching and modeling**    | Modeling mode: partners' taps are said but not added to the message; Find word shows the path to any word, step by step. Hidden words for staged teaching.                                                                                                                                                      | Training materials for partners.                                                                     |
| **Data collection**          | None. Open AAC collects nothing, by design.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Opt-in, on-device language logging (planned for phase 4).                                            |
| **Backup and sharing**       | One-file backups of boards, pictures and settings; Open Board Format import and export; print boards to PDF.                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Sync between devices.                                                                                |
| **Portability and cost**     | Free and open source (AGPL); runs in a browser on iPad, iPhone, Android, Chromebook, Windows and Mac; works offline once installed; can be self-hosted.                                                                                                                                                         | Dedicated hardware; insurance funding routes.                                                        |
| **Response time**            | Words speak from prepared audio, measured at 20–45 ms per tap after preparation, and 75 ms for a new word on an iPhone ([decision 0001](research/decisions/0001-offline-voice.md)).                                                                                                                             | Testing on older and low-end devices.                                                                |
